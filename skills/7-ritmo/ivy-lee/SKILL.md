# Ivy Lee

Six items, ranked, worked strictly in order, unfinished ones carried to
tomorrow's six. The limit is the method: a list you cannot hold in your
head is a list you will skim, and a list you skim gets its items opened
in parallel.

## Steps

1. **Cut to six.** Rank by importance; everything below six goes to a
   holding list, not to the plan. If six feels too few, the items are too
   small — merge them.
   _Done when_: the plan has at most six lines, each with its check.

2. **One at a time.** Work the first to its check before reading the
   second. If blocked, write the block and move to the second — the first
   stays first tomorrow.
   _Done when_: each item is closed, blocked-with-reason, or not started.

3. **Carry, don't grow.** At close, unfinished items head tomorrow's list;
   new items compete for the remaining slots.
   _Done when_: tomorrow's six are written, unfinished first.
